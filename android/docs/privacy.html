DARK WEB LOCK — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Dark Web Lock helps you see when your Bluetooth audio devices connect and disconnect, and optionally where your phone was at that moment, so you can find devices you have left behind.

What the app stores

All data stays on your phone. The app has no account, no server, and does not transmit your data anywhere.

  • Owner details you type (name, optional contact).
  • Paired device details read from Android.
  • Connection history (connect/disconnect times).
  • Location (only if you enable "Save where you last had them"): the phone's location at a connect/disconnect event, stored locally. It is the phone's location, not the device's.

Permissions and why

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): read paired devices, connection status, and scan. Not used for location.
  • Location (optional): only to record where your phone was at a connect/disconnect event, when you enable it.
  • Notifications: the required monitoring notice and left-behind alerts.

What the app does not do

  • It does not upload, sell, or share your data with anyone.
  • It does not track the Bluetooth devices themselves, only your phone.
  • It does not control, lock, or disable any device.

Deleting your data

Clear the history, remove your owner details, or uninstall the app.
