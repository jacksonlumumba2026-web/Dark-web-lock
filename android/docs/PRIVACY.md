# Dark Web Lock — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-13_

Dark Web Lock helps you see when your Bluetooth audio devices connect and
disconnect, and optionally where your phone was at that moment, so you can find
devices you have left behind.

## What the app stores

All data stays **on your phone**. The app has no account, no server, and does
not transmit your data anywhere.

- **Owner details** you type (name, optional contact): stored locally so your
  ownership record shows them.
- **Paired device details** read from Android (name, address, type, class,
  Bluetooth services): shown in the app and included only in a record you
  choose to share.
- **Connection history** (times a device connected or disconnected): stored
  locally.
- **Location** (only if you turn on "Save where you last had them"): the
  phone's location captured at a connect/disconnect event, stored locally so
  you can open it in a map. It is the phone's location, not the device's.

## Permissions and why

- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth):** to read paired devices, their connection
  status, and to scan. Not used for location.
- **Location (optional):** only to record where your phone was when a device
  connected or disconnected, when you enable that feature. You can turn it off
  at any time.
- **Notifications:** to show the required monitoring notice and left-behind
  alerts.

## What the app does NOT do

- It does not upload, sell, or share your data with anyone.
- It does not track the Bluetooth devices themselves, only your phone.
- It does not control, lock, or disable any device.

## Sharing

The app never shares data on its own. If you tap a "Share" button, you choose
where that text goes; that is under your control.

## Deleting your data

Clear the connection history in the app, remove your owner details, or uninstall
the app to remove all stored data.
